Access and dependent invitations

Why can’t I create a household?

In the public release, an adult age 18 or older signs in with Apple, confirms the current Terms and Privacy Policy, and creates one household. Creation can be unavailable while a prior deletion is still completing, after repeated security checks, or when the service cannot verify the signed-in account or app. Confirm the app is current, reconnect, and try again. If the message remains, contact support without sending a password, access code, or private household content.

How does a dependent join?

A household adult completes the age and privacy confirmation in the app, creates a short-lived, single-use invitation, and opens it on the dependent’s device. Never share an invitation publicly.

The first release does not permit linking a child under 13. A household adult should contact support before creating or linking a profile if age eligibility is unclear.

My invitation expired.

Ask a household adult to create a new invitation. Expired or already-used invitations cannot be reopened.

Task agreements

What happens after a task is sent?

The recipient can accept, decline, or request a structured change to the time budget, due time, reward, or note. Acceptance records the agreement; it does not start the timer unless the recipient chooses Accept & Start.

When is a reward recorded?

Assigned work is submitted for review. The household reward posts only after the assigner approves completion. A self-started task can be completed directly.

Does a money reward transfer funds?

No. Money rewards are private household ledger promises only. Simpli-FI Family does not move money, connect to bank accounts, or provide financial advice.

Timers and synchronization

Why did the timer continue past its budget?

The time budget is a target, not an automatic stop. Continuing records overtime so the household can understand how long the work actually took.

Why does another device show a slightly different number?

Give the devices a moment to reconnect. The service uses authoritative timestamps rather than sending every ticking second. If the difference remains, close and reopen the relevant task while connected to the internet.

Can I run two timers at once?

No. Simpli-FI Family allows one active task timer per family member so the daily record remains understandable.

Apple Watch

How do I connect the Watch app?

  1. Sign in on the paired iPhone first.
  2. Install Simpli-FI Family from the Watch app on iPhone if it was not installed automatically.
  3. Open the iPhone app, then open Simpli-FI Family on Apple Watch.

The first release uses the paired iPhone for authentication and authoritative synchronization.

What if my Watch is offline?

Supported task actions can wait on the Watch and reconcile after the paired iPhone reconnects. Keep both apps installed and avoid signing into a different household while commands are waiting.

Notifications

Notifications are optional. If they are off, assignments and timer records still appear when you open the app.

Change notification permission

Open the Settings app on iPhone or iPad, choose Notifications, then choose Simpli-FI Family. Notification previews are designed not to include task names, private notes, or reward details.

Account controls

Sign out

Open More, choose Sign out, and confirm. Signing out removes the active session from that device.

Delete an account or household

Open More, choose Delete account, read the effect carefully, and confirm. A primary owner must sign in with Apple again if the last authentication is more than five minutes old. A successful primary-owner request revokes access and removes the household, its active records, managed-dependent accounts, and the owner account.

A non-owner adult request removes that person’s login and household membership without deleting the shared household. A dependent request removes the linked login, dependent account, and membership records without deleting the rest of the household. An adult may create a new managed profile later if appropriate. A pseudonymous retry receipt may remain for up to 30 days.

Safety and household access

If a task, private note, or change request makes you feel unsafe, you can sign out immediately or delete your account from More. A household adult can also revoke a linked dependent device.

To report a safety concern, do not copy private task text, access codes, passwords, or unnecessary information about a child. Tell us the affected account role, device type, and what help you need.
