Privacy policy

Family data should stay family data.

Effective July 15, 2026 · Last updated July 16, 2026

This policy explains what Simpli-FI Family collects, why it is needed, and the controls available to adults and household members.

Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the Simpli-FI Family application and its related support pages. Simpli-FI Family is operated by Simpli-FI OS LLC, a Texas limited liability company based in Denton County, Texas, United States. In this policy, “Simpli-FI,” “we,” “us,” and “our” refer to Simpli-FI OS LLC.

Simpli-FI Family is an invitation-based family productivity service for task agreements, time budgeting, timers, household rewards, and personal history.

The first release is intended only for invited users in the United States.

The app is not a public social network, banking service, money-transfer service, or advertising platform.

Information we collect

Account and household information

When an adult signs in with Apple, we receive an Apple authentication credential and may receive a name and email address or Apple private-relay address. We create service identifiers needed to authenticate the account, authorize household access, and link invited dependents. For a linked dependent, we store a coarse 13-or-older eligibility band and the adult’s attestation metadata, not a birth date.

Content you choose to create

We store information needed for the features you use, including display names; task titles and categories; goals, projects, routines, and focus sessions; time budgets; due times; private notes; assignment responses; structured change requests; timer sessions; completion status; quest points; custom rewards; and household money-ledger entries.

If you choose to create fitness, exercise, meal-planning, nutrition, or hydration goals or tasks, we process that user-provided fitness and wellness information as private Household Content so it can appear in the relevant household plan. The first release does not connect to HealthKit or request clinical records, diagnoses, biometrics, medical measurements, or data from motion-and-fitness sensors.

Adult-only finance and email summaries

If a household adult uses the finance snapshot, we store the aggregate amounts that adult enters, such as total assets, liabilities, monthly income and expenses, debt progress, and subscription count. The snapshot does not require bank credentials, account numbers, card numbers, statements, or transactions.

If the connected-email summary is enabled and a household adult chooses to connect an inbox, we process the provider name, sender display name, subject, received time, and unread status needed to show up to three recent messages. The Family dashboard does not request or display message bodies, snippets, attachments, email addresses, or provider message identifiers. Provider authorization credentials are handled server-side and are not exposed to other household members.

Device and notification information

If you enable relevant features, we process app-installation identifiers, push-notification tokens, paired-device state, queued command identifiers, and limited operational information needed to deliver notifications and keep iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch surfaces synchronized. In version 1.0, Live Activities are updated locally on the device; we do not request or store ActivityKit push tokens or remotely update Live Activities.

Approximate location for weather

If you choose “Use my location” on Home, the app requests location while in use, reduces the coordinate to neighborhood-scale precision on the device, and sends that reduced coordinate to Apple Weather so it can show today’s local conditions. Simpli-FI Family does not request background location or store a location history in the household service.

Reliability and diagnostic information

Bundled Firebase and Google components may process limited, pseudonymous or non-linked installation, messaging, transport, and diagnostic information for app functionality, reliability, and operational analytics. We do not use this information to profile household content, advertise to family members, or track people across other companies’ apps or websites.

Support communications

If you contact us, we receive the information you include in the message. Please do not send passwords, access codes, private task notes, or unnecessary information about a child.

Simpli-FI Family does not request contacts, photos, retained microphone recordings, HealthKit data, clinical records, diagnoses, biometrics, medical measurements, bank credentials, account numbers, or payment-card details for its 1.0 features. Dictation, keyboard, and Scribble input are provided through Apple’s system interfaces and are subject to your device and Apple settings.

How we use information

We use the information described above to:

  • authenticate adults and enforce invitation-based household access;
  • create and synchronize task agreements, timers, completion reviews, and household reward records;
  • deliver requested notifications and active-timer surfaces;
  • show adult-requested finance snapshots, connected-email metadata, and local weather;
  • prevent unauthorized access, replayed commands, abuse, and cross-household data exposure;
  • respond to support, deletion, privacy, and security requests; and
  • maintain the reliability, security, and accessibility of the service using privacy-minimized operational information.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use household data for third-party advertising, data brokerage, or cross-app tracking.

Service providers and sharing

We disclose information only as needed to operate the service, comply with law, or protect people and the service.

Apple

Apple provides Sign in with Apple, Apple Weather, App Store distribution, push-notification delivery, device operating systems, and system input surfaces. Apple processes information under its own policies and your Apple settings.

Firebase and Google Cloud

Firebase and Google Cloud provide authentication support, database storage, server functions, app-integrity checks, notification routing, and limited operational diagnostics. Data is processed for Simpli-FI under applicable service terms and access controls.

Public pages, invitation fallback, and support email

The public Family, support, privacy, terms, and security pages are static files delivered through Vercel and related website-hosting and network providers. The pages do not include analytics, advertising, or third-party font scripts. Basic requests, including an IP address, device and browser information, and the requested URL, may be processed in standard delivery and security logs under those providers’ service terms.

If an invitation does not open directly in the installed app, its short-lived token reaches the web host as part of the requested URL. The invitation page removes the query from browser history before showing invitation state and does not place the token in the page, browser storage, analytics, console output, or a link. Invitation tokens are designed to expire and work once. If you email support, your email provider and ours process the message for delivery and response.

Connected email providers

If connected-email summaries are enabled and an adult authorizes a provider, that provider processes the authorization and metadata request under its own terms and privacy policy. Simpli-FI requests only the limited permission needed for the enabled summary and does not use connected email for advertising.

Legal and safety disclosures

We may preserve or disclose limited information if reasonably necessary to comply with law, respond to lawful process, enforce our agreements, investigate security incidents, or protect the rights and safety of users or others.

Household visibility

Household content is not public. Household adults can view household membership and household task, timer, completion-review, and reward records. Assigners and assignees can view their shared agreement, event history, and structured change requests. Dependents can view their own timer and reward records according to their role.

The service uses authenticated, role-based access controls. It does not claim that household content is end-to-end encrypted.

Dependents and children

Simpli-FI Family is not offered for public child self-signup. In the first release, dependent-device linking is limited to family members age 13 or older. Do not create or link an account for a child under 13.

We limit dependent access according to household role. Dependents do not receive public profiles, public discovery, unrestricted messaging, advertising, or banking features.

If we learn that we collected personal information online from a child under 13 without legally sufficient authorization, we will suspend the relevant access, investigate, and delete the information as required. A household adult should contact us immediately if a child under 13 was linked or if a dependent was linked without appropriate adult authorization.

Retention and deletion

We retain account and household information while the account is active and as needed to provide the service, secure it, meet legal obligations, and resolve disputes. Adults can start account or household deletion from the app.

A successful primary-owner deletion revokes household access and removes the active household tree, managed-dependent accounts, and owner account as part of the server request. A non-owner adult deletion removes that person’s account and membership without deleting records the household must retain as its shared task history. A dependent self-deletion removes the linked login, household membership records, and dependent account; an adult may create a new managed profile later if appropriate.

A pseudonymous deletion receipt, keyed by a one-way user hash and containing the deletion mode, relevant household identifiers, and request state, may be retained for up to 30 days to make interrupted requests safe to retry. Versioned legal-acknowledgement receipts contain the account identifier, policy versions, acceptance flow, app version/build, server acceptance time, and—when applicable—the managed profile and authorizing adult identifiers. Those receipts may be retained for up to four years to establish consent and enforce the agreement.

Privacy-minimized security, authorization, command, notification, and reliability records are retained only for the period reasonably needed for those purposes, legal compliance, or dispute resolution. Residual copies may remain temporarily in protected provider backups until those backups rotate under the provider’s standard schedule.

Your choices, rights, and appeals

  • Notifications: choose whether to allow notifications in Apple device settings.
  • Household content: create, respond to, complete, and review tasks according to your household role.
  • Device access: household adults can revoke linked dependent-device access.
  • Sign out: remove the active session from a device in the app.
  • Deletion: start account or household deletion in the app or contact us for help.

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to confirm whether we process your personal information; access, correct, delete, or obtain a portable copy of it; and opt out of a sale, targeted advertising, or profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects. We do not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising or this kind of profiling. We will not discriminate against you for exercising a privacy right.

To exercise a right, email ready@simpli-fi-os.com and identify the request as relating to Simpli-FI Family. We will acknowledge and respond within the period required by applicable law. We may need to verify the requester and the requester’s authority over the household before acting. A teen may submit a request about the teen’s own account. A household adult or another authorized agent may submit a request when applicable law and the person’s authority permit, but we may ask for proof of authority and direct identity confirmation.

If we deny a privacy request, reply to our decision within 30 days with the word “Appeal” in the subject line and explain why you believe the decision should be reconsidered. We will review the appeal and explain the result. Texas residents may contact the Texas Attorney General if an appeal is denied.

You can withdraw optional feature permissions through Apple device settings, disconnect an enabled email provider, revoke a dependent device, sign out, or request deletion. Withdrawal does not affect processing that was lawful before the withdrawal or information we must retain for legal, security, or dispute-resolution purposes.

Security and international processing

We use administrative and technical safeguards designed to restrict data to authorized household members and service operations. These include authenticated access, role checks, server-authoritative state changes, app-integrity controls, and minimized notification content. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Read our Security & Data Practices page for more information and instructions for reporting a suspected vulnerability.

Service providers may process information in the United States and other locations where they operate. Where required, we use contractual and technical measures intended to protect that processing.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the product, service providers, or legal requirements change. We will post the revised policy here with an updated effective or last-updated date and provide additional notice when a material change requires it.

Contact us

For privacy questions, dependent-access concerns, or deletion support, email ready@simpli-fi-os.com.
