Less reminding.
More agreement.

A task should not become a negotiation after it has already started. Simpli-FI Family makes the agreement visible first, then keeps the timer and completion record simple.

01

Choose the work

Pick from a curated task library, household favorites, or something used recently. New tasks are easy to save for next time.

02

Make the agreement

Select one family member, set the time budget and optional due time, then choose no reward, quest points, a household money-ledger entry, or a custom reward.

03

Respond with respect

The recipient can accept, decline, or request a structured change. Acceptance can stay separate from starting the timer.

04

Track the real time

Start, pause, resume, and complete from iPhone, iPad, or a paired Apple Watch. Overtime stays in the record instead of disappearing.

Clear by design

Built for a household, not an audience.

Family coordination is personal. The product is adult led, invitation based, and intentionally free of public profiles, open-ended chat, advertising, and cross-app tracking.

Adult-led access

Adults create invited households. Dependents use parent-managed membership instead of public self-signup.

Private work

Tasks, notes, responses, timers, and household rewards stay within authorized household access.

No attention market

No ads, public feeds, searchable profiles, or third-party advertising use.
