The Builder

Built by a firefighter.
Designed for operators.

Simpli-FI OS is the creation of Benjamin "Hunter" Lott — a Fire Captain with the City of Denton, a Registered Investment Adviser, and a builder who can't stop solving problems.

Every project in this portfolio was born from a real gap: recruiting platforms that don't serve first responders, compliance tools that don't speak plain English, local economies that can't find each other, and churches that don't know who in their congregation can help.

We don't build software for the sake of building software. We build it because the friction is unacceptable, and nobody else is fixing it.
